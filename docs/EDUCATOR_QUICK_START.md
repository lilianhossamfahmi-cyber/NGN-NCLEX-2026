# Quick Start Guide for Educators
**Master NGN Question Creator v2.0**

Welcome! This tool helps you create high-quality, copyright-safe NCLEX-Next Gen questions in seconds.

---

## 1. Getting Started
Open the application in your browser. You will see the **Global Reference Library** at the top.

### Uploading References (Optional but Recommended)
*   Click **"+ Upload Reference"** (Blue Button).
*   Select your course PDF, guideline doc, or CSV.
*   **Result**: The file appears in your library. Check the box to make it "Active". The AI will now use this document to check facts!

## 2. Creating Questions
There are 3 main modes to create questions:

*   **Upload Mode**: Uses *only* your uploaded files. Best for strict textbook alignment.
*   **Manual Mode**: You paste a case study (vitals, history). Best for specific clinical scenarios you encountered.
*   **AI Mode**: You just verify a prompt (e.g., "Sepsis in a 78yo female"). Best for speed.
*   **Hybrid Mode (Pro)**: Combines all three!

### Step-by-Step Generation
1.  **Select Hybrid Mode** (Recommended).
2.  **Paste Context**: Copy/paste your patient's vitals or history into the Manual Data box.
3.  **Choose Targets**: In the right panel, check **Case Study (6-Screen)** and **Stand-Alone Trend**.
4.  **Set Quantity**: Slide the bar to "2" (This generates 2 of each type = 4 items total).
5.  **Set Focus**: Select "Critical Care" from the dropdown.
6.  **Click "Generate Questions"**.

## 3. Reviewing & Editing
After 10-20 seconds, your items appear in the **Results Grid**.
*   **Green Score (85-100)**: Excellent quality, likely ready to use.
*   **Amber Score (70-84)**: Good, but verify the distractors.
*   **Red Score (<70)**: Needs review.

Click **"Review & Edit"** on any card to open the editor. You can change text, values, and correct answers. Autosave is on!

## 4. Exporting
Once satisfied:
1.  Click **"Export All"** at the top right.
2.  Choose **PDF** for printing or **JSON** for your LMS.
3.  The file downloads to your computer.

---

**Tip**: Use the "Temperature" slider to control creativity. Set it to 0.5 (Conservative) for strict exams, or 1.5 (Creative) for practice drills.
