# Wellness Exercises Library (Content)

This file contains the complete content for the 12 Stress Relief exercises used in the Wellness Module.

## 1. Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
- **Duration**: 3-5 min
- **Difficulty**: 1/5
- **Effectiveness**: 4.6/5
- **Category**: Breathing
- **When to use**: High anxiety, Panic, Pre-exam
- **Instructions**:
  1. Sit comfortably with your back straight.
  2. Exhale completely, emptying your lungs.
  3. Inhale through your nose deeply for **4 seconds**.
  4. Hold your breath for **4 seconds**.
  5. Exhale through your mouth for **4 seconds**.
  6. Hold your empty lungs for **4 seconds**.
  7. Repeat this cycle for at least 5 rounds.
- **Benefits**: Lowers cortisol immediately, activates parasympathetic system.

## 2. Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)
- **Duration**: 10-15 min
- **Difficulty**: 2/5
- **Effectiveness**: 4.3/5
- **Category**: Somatic
- **When to use**: Physical tension, Evening wind-down
- **Instructions**:
  1. Find a comfortable position lying down or sitting.
  2. Start with your **toes**: Curl them tight for 5s, then release instantly. Feel the tension leave.
  3. Move to **calves**: Flex hard for 5s, release for 5s.
  4. Continue up the body: Thighs, Buttocks, Stomach, Hands, Arms, Shoulders, Face.
  5. End by tensing entire body for 5s, then releasing into total relaxation.
- **Benefits**: Physical release of stress stored in muscles.

## 3. Guided Imagery / Visualization
- **Duration**: 5-10 min
- **Difficulty**: 2/5
- **Effectiveness**: 4.4/5
- **Category**: Mindfulness
- **When to use**: Confidence boost, Anxiety
- **Instructions**:
  1. Close your eyes and take three deep breaths.
  2. Visualize a "Safe Place" (beach, forest, room).
  3. Engage senses: What do you see? Hear? Smell?
  4. Now visualize yourself taking the NCLEX. See yourself calm.
  5. See yourself answering confidently. Visualize the "Pass" result.
  6. Anchor this feeling of success.
- **Benefits**: Mental rehearsal improves actual performance (sports psychology).

## 4. Body Scan Meditation
- **Duration**: 10-20 min
- **Difficulty**: 2/5
- **Effectiveness**: 4.5/5
- **Category**: Mindfulness
- **When to use**: Insomnia, Deep rest
- **Instructions**:
  1. Lie on your back, arms at sides.
  2. Bring attention to your toes. Don't move them, just notice sensations.
  3. Slowly move attention up to ankles, shins, knees.
  4. If you find tension, visualize breath flowing into that area.
  5. Continue scanning up to the top of your head.
  6. Rest in the awareness of your whole body.
- **Benefits**: Improves sleep quality, breaks cycle of ruminating thoughts.

## 5. 4-7-8 Breathing
- **Duration**: 5 min
- **Difficulty**: 2/5
- **Effectiveness**: 4.4/5
- **Category**: Breathing
- **When to use**: Insomnia, Acute stress spike
- **Instructions**:
  1. Place tongue tip behind upper front teeth.
  2. Exhale completely with a "whoosh".
  3. Inhale quiet through nose for **4**.
  4. Hold breath for **7**.
  5. Exhale audible through mouth for **8**.
  6. Repeat 4 times.
- **Benefits**: Natural tranquilizer for the nervous system.

## 6. Grounding (5-4-3-2-1)
- **Duration**: 5 min
- **Difficulty**: 1/5
- **Effectiveness**: 4.7/5
- **Category**: Grounding
- **When to use**: Panic attack, Dissociation
- **Instructions**:
  1. Acknowledge **5** things you see.
  2. Acknowledge **4** things you can touch.
  3. Acknowledge **3** things you hear.
  4. Acknowledge **2** things you can smell.
  5. Acknowledge **1** thing you can taste.
- **Benefits**: Pulls brain out of "flight" mode and back to reality.

## 7. Gentle Stretching
- **Duration**: 10-15 min
- **Difficulty**: 2/5
- **Category**: Movement
- **When to use**: Mid-study break, Stiffness
- **Instructions**:
  1. Neck Rolls (30s)
  2. Shoulder Shrugs (30s)
  3. Seated Twist (Left/Right)
  4. Wrist Circles (important for computer work)
  5. Standing Forward Fold
- **Benefits**: Increases blood flow to brain, reduces fatigue.

## 8. Positive Affirmations
- **Duration**: 3 min
- **Difficulty**: 1/5
- **Category**: Cognitive
- **When to use**: Self-doubt, Morning prep
- **Instructions**:
  1. Stand in "Power Pose" (hands on hips) or sit tall.
  2. Repeat aloud: "I am capable and prepared."
  3. Repeat: "I trust my hard work."
  4. Repeat: "I will be a safe and effective nurse."
- **Benefits**: Rewires negative thought patterns.

## 9. Nature Walk
- **Duration**: 15-30 min
- **Difficulty**: 1/5
- **Category**: Movement/Nature
- **When to use**: Burnout, Long session break
- **Instructions**:
  1. Leave phone behind (or on DND).
  2. Go outside.
  3. Walk at a relaxed pace.
  4. Focus entirely on surroundings (trees, sky, wind).
- **Benefits**: Nature exposure lowers cortisol significantly.

## 10. Cold Water Splash
- **Duration**: 1 min
- **Difficulty**: 1/5
- **Category**: Somatic
- **When to use**: Panic, Sleepiness
- **Instructions**:
  1. Fill sink or bowl with ice-cold water.
  2. Splash face vigorously or submerge face for 10-15s.
  3. Pat dry.
- **Benefits**: Triggers "Mammalian Dive Reflex" which slows heart rate immediately.

## 11. Gratitude Journaling
- **Duration**: 5-10 min
- **Difficulty**: 1/5
- **Category**: Cognitive
- **When to use**: Evening reflection, Negative mood
- **Instructions**:
  1. Write down 3 things you are grateful for today.
  2. Write 1 thing you accomplished.
  3. Write 1 intention for tomorrow.
- **Benefits**: Shifts focus from "lack" to "abundance", improves mood.

## 12. Laugh & Fun Break
- **Duration**: 5-10 min
- **Difficulty**: 1/5
- **Category**: Social/Emotional
- **When to use**: Heavy mood, Burnout
- **Instructions**:
  1. Stop studying.
  2. Watch a specific funny video, call a funny friend, or read a comic.
  3. Allow yourself to laugh out loud.
- **Benefits**: Endorphin release counters stress hormones.
