=== MNEMONIC MAPPING RULES (CRITICAL) ===

NEVER use generic or incorrect mnemonics. Match mnemonics to the actual clinical condition:

- Topic: "Sepsis bundle" → Mnemonic: "Sepsis-6" or "Hour-1 Bundle"

- Topic: "Myocardial Infarction" → Mnemonic: "MONA"
- Topic: "Heart Failure" → Mnemonic: "LMNOP"

ALWAYS ensure the mnemonic matches the PRIMARY condition, not associated symptoms.
